## Questions for the owner

1. **Is TAB Motors Arlington related to tabmotors.com?** That site lists this address and phone beside the DC shop, but this website's footer says it is not affiliated with any similarly named business. If related, the footer needs changing and a link from tabmotors.com is the fastest strong link available. Sat 8-2 and the 2-year warranty there conflict with Sat 7-3 and the 1-year warranty here. If unrelated, ask them to remove the address.
2. **Which name is official?** The site says "TAB Motors Arlington". Google says "TAB Motors Auto Repair Arlington". Pick one for listings. Check it against the legal or signage name, because Google's guidelines do not allow keywords that are not part of the real name.
3. **Is the shop a DEQ Certified Emissions Repair Facility?** If yes, the emissions pages should say so, since only certified repairs count toward a waiver.
4. **What high-voltage hybrid or EV work does the shop do?** The site says it services hybrids and EVs. The guide tells owners to call about high-voltage jobs until this is confirmed.
5. **Can the review count stay accurate?** Google showed 75 on October 27 and 72 on November 4. Site updated to 72. Birdeye shows 272 Google reviews for this address, which could not be matched to any Google listing.
